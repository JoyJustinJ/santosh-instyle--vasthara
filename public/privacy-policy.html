This Privacy Policy describes how Santhosh Silks ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Vasthara mobile application ("App"). The App is designed to help customers of Santhosh Silks manage their savings schemes, chit funds, and payment history.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

📋 1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Phone Number: Required for OTP-based login and account identification
  • Name: Used to personalise your account and scheme records
  • Google Account details: If you sign in with Google (name, email, profile photo)

1.2 Transaction & Financial Data

  • Savings scheme enrollment and contribution records
  • Payment history (amounts, dates, plan names)
  • Due dates and installment information

1.3 Technical Data

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version and session logs (via Firebase)
  • Crash reports for bug fixes

🎯 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Authenticate your identity and provide secure access to your account
  • Display your active savings schemes, payment history, and due amounts
  • Send OTP verification messages for login
  • Process payments through Razorpay
  • Generate and share payment receipts and statements
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs
  • Contact you about your account when necessary

🤝 3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We share data only with:

  • Firebase (Google): For authentication, database storage, and crash reporting
  • Razorpay: For processing payments — subject to Razorpay's Privacy Policy
  • Pay4SMS / SMS Gateway: For delivering OTP messages to your phone
  • Law enforcement: If required by law or court order
⚠️ We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing purposes.

🔐 4. Authentication & Security

  • Phone OTP: We use your phone number to send a one-time password for secure login via Firebase Authentication
  • Google Sign-In: OAuth 2.0 is used; we only receive your name, email, and profile photo from Google
  • Data Encryption: All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS
  • Firebase Security Rules: Your data is accessible only to your authenticated account

💳 5. Payment Processing

Payments in the App are processed by Razorpay, a PCI DSS-compliant payment gateway. We do not store your credit/debit card numbers or bank account details on our servers. Payment confirmations and transaction IDs are stored in our system for record-keeping.

👆 6. Biometric Data

If you enable biometric login (fingerprint or face recognition), this feature uses your device's built-in biometric authentication system.

We never access, collect, or store your biometric data. The authentication happens entirely on your device using the Android Biometric API. We only receive a pass/fail result.

🗓️ 7. Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained while your account is active
  • Financial records: Retained for 7 years for legal and audit compliance
  • Crash logs: Retained for 90 days
  • Deleted accounts: Personal data deleted within 30 days of account deletion request

⚖️ 8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Opt-out: Disable non-essential notifications at any time via App settings

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

👶 9. Children's Privacy

The Vasthara App is intended for users 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If we become aware that a child under 18 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

📝 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. For significant changes, we will notify you via the App or SMS.
